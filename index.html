Teacher Roblox Game uploader

A private tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel Teacher Roblox Game (@teacher_roblox_game) to publish the channel's own daily quiz videos. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it.

What it does

  • Uploads the channel's own quiz videos (made on the owner's computer) to that same channel, with their title, description and tags, and schedules when each one becomes public.
  • Posts the channel's own comments under the channel's own videos (the day's bonus question), and the channel's replies to viewers' comments there.
  • Reads the public comments under the channel's own bonus-question videos to find the first three correct answers, whose YouTube display names are then thanked ("shoutout") in a later video. There are no prizes.

It uses the YouTube API Services. By using or interacting with it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Privacy policy

Effective 15 November 2026.

Who we are

The tool is run by the channel's owner. Questions: cpaone.mail@gmail.com.

Data we access

  • The owner's YouTube account, through Google sign-in (OAuth), with permission to upload videos and to post comments and replies. Only the channel owner signs in. The sign-in token is kept on the owner's computer and is never shared.
  • Public comments under the channel's own bonus-question videos: the commenter's public display name, the comment text and when it was posted, read through the YouTube Data API.

The same bonus question is also posted on the channel's TikTok account, where comments are checked in the same way and for the same purpose. That part does not use the YouTube API Services.

How we use it

  • The owner's authorization is used only to upload and schedule the channel's own videos and to post the channel's own comments and replies.
  • Comments are read only to check answers to the bonus question. The display names of up to three people who answered correctly first are shown in a later video and its description, as a public thank-you.
  • We do not use any of this data for advertising, profiling or any other purpose, and we never sell or share it.

Storage and deletion

  • Everything is stored only on the owner's own computer: the sign-in token, a list of uploaded videos, and the winners' display names with the comment checks used to pick them. Nothing is stored on any other server.
  • Comment checks are kept only as a record of how each bonus was decided, and are deleted on request. Winners' display names remain in the published videos.
  • If you would like your name removed from a future shoutout, or your data deleted, email cpaone.mail@gmail.com and we will do it within 7 days.
  • The channel owner can withdraw the tool's access at any time at Google Account → Third-party connections (also listed under security settings).

Cookies

The tool has no website of its own beyond this page. It does not place cookies or tracking on any device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version and its date will be posted on this page.
